Privacy policy

Effective 26 October 2026 · Developer: Sergii Tsiapenko

LabelFolio does not require an app account. The app has no developer-operated backend, advertising, analytics or tracking.

Label designs and imported files

Your label designs, imported images and named labels are stored locally by the app. LabelFolio reads files you select, open or drop into it. The developer does not receive your label content, imported images, QR payloads or usage events through the app.

Portable .labelfolio documents include their images and design data. Exported files are written to a destination you choose. If you save to a cloud-managed folder or share a file, that provider’s privacy practices apply.

Bluetooth and printer information

Bluetooth is used to discover compatible printers, read available model and status information, reconnect to your last-used printer and send labels you choose to print. Known-printer information and diagnostic logs are kept on your device. The app does not send Bluetooth device identifiers or printer diagnostics to the developer automatically.

Printing sends the selected label data to your chosen printer. Printer hardware and firmware are provided by their manufacturers.

Optional writing suggestions

The optional Write feature uses Apple’s on-device Foundation Models on eligible devices. LabelFolio does not send your writing prompts to a developer-operated server. Manual editing and printing do not require Apple Intelligence. Apple’s system features are governed by Apple’s own privacy practices.

Your choices and data removal

You can delete saved designs in My Labels, remove exported files using Finder, and revoke Bluetooth access in macOS System Settings. Export anything you want to keep before removing the app’s local data. Deleting a saved label does not delete files you exported elsewhere.

Support you choose to request

If you contact the developer or share diagnostics, only provide the information needed to resolve the issue. The developer uses information you voluntarily provide to respond and troubleshoot. Public GitHub issues and any files posted there are visible to others and handled under GitHub’s terms and privacy practices. Never post sensitive label contents or credentials.

This website

This website does not add analytics, tracking scripts, advertising or cookies. It is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, as described in its GitHub Pages documentation and privacy statement. GitHub’s website and support services operate separately from the LabelFolio app.

Contact and updates

For support, private privacy requests or questions about this policy, email tsdeveloper@pm.me or visit the LabelFolio support page. Do not post private information in a public issue. This page will be updated if LabelFolio’s data practices change; the effective date appears above.
