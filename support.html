A little help

Let’s get your label ready.

Setup guidance and answers for LabelFolio on Apple silicon Macs running macOS 14 or later.

Connect and print

  1. Turn on the printer and load compatible thermal label stock. Close other apps that may be holding its Bluetooth connection.
  2. Allow LabelFolio to use Bluetooth when macOS asks. The app looks for nearby supported printers while it is open.
  3. Use Printer in the bottom strip to choose a device and check the detected model. Your last-used printer can reconnect automatically.
  4. Set Label size to match your paper, create your design, then choose Print label. Review the preview before confirming the print.

Printer compatibility

Physically tested: NIIMBOT B1 Pro monochrome printing over Bluetooth.

Additional NIIMBOT and other manufacturer profiles are experimental until tested with the specific hardware. A printer appearing in discovery does not guarantee support for printing. Model, firmware, Bluetooth implementation and label stock can affect compatibility.

Black/red output requires compatible printer hardware and stock and has not been physically verified. A printer and paper are not included with the app.

Common questions

My printer is visible but will not connect.

Make sure it is awake, nearby and not connected to another phone or app. Check LabelFolio’s access in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Bluetooth. Restart the printer, then select it in the Printer menu. After a deliberate disconnect, select it manually or relaunch LabelFolio to resume automatic reconnection.

A print timed out or reported a protocol error.

Check the paper before retrying: the printer may already have printed the label. Confirm the model and loaded stock. Open Help → Diagnostic to inspect the connection details. Share only the relevant lines after checking them for personal information.

Why is the printable width smaller than the paper?

The thermal print head may be narrower than the label stock. Use the paper’s physical size for the label and the detected printer’s printable area for artwork. Content outside that area is clipped when printed.

How can I make photos look better?

Select the image, choose Photo mode and adjust brightness and contrast. Dithering represents shading with black dots. Artwork / QR mode suits high-contrast graphics. Crop tightly around your subject and use a larger placement for more detail; a thermal printer cannot reproduce full-colour photo quality.

My QR code is difficult to scan.

Keep a clear white margin around it, avoid overlapping other artwork, and increase its physical size. Shorter content usually creates a simpler code. Test the printed result with the scanner you intend to use, especially with decorative styles.

How do I keep the exact size when exporting?

Set the label dimensions first, then export a PDF. Its page matches those dimensions. When printing the PDF from another application, choose actual size or 100% and matching paper; a “fit to page” option changes the physical size.

Where are my designs stored?

My Labels stores named designs locally. Save a portable .labelfolio document to a location you choose for backup or sharing. Images are embedded in the document. Files saved to a cloud-managed folder follow that provider’s settings.

Why is the Write tab unavailable?

Select a single Text element. Writing suggestions require an eligible Mac, macOS 26 or later, Apple Intelligence enabled and available on-device models. All manual editing tools work without Apple Intelligence.

Need more help?

Email tsdeveloper@pm.me for support or private privacy requests. Include your app version, macOS version and printer model where relevant.

Report a problem or request a feature in the LabelFolio issue tracker. Include your app version, macOS version, printer model and firmware if known, plus the steps that reproduce the problem.
